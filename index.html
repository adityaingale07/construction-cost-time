How Was This Estimate Calculated?

Transparent Civil Engineering quantity formulas

1. Total Built-up Area Calculation 📐 GEOMETRY
Plot Length × Plot Width × Number of Floors
Enter plot dimensions above to calculate built-up area
2. Cement Requirement (Thumb Rule + Wastage) 🧱 MATERIALS
Built-up Area × 0.4 bags/sq.ft × (1 + 5% wastage)
Enter dimensions and cement procurement rate to calculate
3. Steel Reinforcement (TMT Rebars) ⚙ MATERIALS
Built-up Area × 4 kg/sq.ft × (1 + 5% wastage)
Enter dimensions and steel procurement rate to calculate
4. Labour Cost Computation 👷 MANPOWER
Rate Rule: Built-up Area × Labour Rate
Enter labour rate or percentage to calculate
5. Grand Construction Cost & Unit Rates 🏆 TOTAL BOQ
Total Cost = Total Material Cost + Labour Cost | Unit Rate = Total Cost ÷ Total Area
Total Material Cost + Labour Cost = Grand Budget
